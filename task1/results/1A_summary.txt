 makes warmup(2)+reps(20)=22 calls to the kernel, so raw sweep_full.csv counts

Instruction counts do not change the FLOP count (same arithmetic), but reorder/
unroll DROP total instruction count vs naive because the K*K accumulate loop no
longer redoes address arithmetic per tap; unroll drops it further (fewer loop-
branch/compare instructions per output). At 2048x2048, reorder/unroll still LOSE
to naive in wall-clock time (0.66-0.72x, see 1D) despite fewer instructions --
they've become memory-bandwidth-bound (confirmed via LLC-miss counters, see
1B_l2_llc_hierarchy.csv: reorder's LLC misses at 2048 are ~55M vs naive's ~266K).
